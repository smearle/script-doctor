\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

World-model research has largely focused on individual, visually rich games, spending capacity on pixels rather than the symbolic mechanics that determine what happens next.
Our rule-conditioned NCA studies the complementary regime: a single dynamics model spanning a family of mechanically distinct, fully discrete environments, light enough to study at the rule level on commodity hardware.
The architecture is not PuzzleScript-specific---any deterministic discrete-grid simulator with declarative rules (Conway's Game of Life, lattice-gas / reaction-diffusion CAs) admits the same pattern.

Downstream applications include sample-efficient policy learning and gradient-based planning across many environments without per-game retraining, fast adaptation on interactive benchmarks~\cite{warrier2025benchmarking,arcprize2026arcagi3} where rule inference from limited interaction is central, and a bridge between neural world models and symbolic program learning via a latent space that doubles as a search space over executable game programs (Appendices~\ref{app:autoencoder},~\ref{app:inverse-fit}; complements program-synthesis approaches~\cite{tang2024worldcoder,ahmed2025theorycoder,ellis2021dreamcoder}).
Our slot encoder is intentionally level-independent, and we restrict to single-step prediction; multi-step training is straightforward but unexplored.

(
  Minimal Collapse-derivative for the adaptive-halt within-game test.
  One rule, one action does work: pressing right makes the player slide
  cell-by-cell until hitting a wall (engine resolves the `again` loop
  within a single tick). Each level places the player at a different
  distance from the right wall, so the same model has to do varying
  amounts of computation per instance to predict the next state.

  The halt head should learn to halt at depth ~= distance for "right"
  transitions, and ~= 1 for any other action (which is a no-op here).
)

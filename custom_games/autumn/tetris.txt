title tetris (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench tetris.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench tetris.sexp to PuzzleScript.  GRID_SIZE 16, realtime.

  Autumn mechanics (empirically probed):
    - Active L-piece (4 cells) falls 1 row per no-input tick.
    - left/right: shift piece 1 column, no gravity that tick.
    - up/down: rotate 90 deg CCW, no gravity.  Idempotent after 1 rotate.
    - At row 15: piece freezes as Block cells, new piece spawns (stochastic).
    - Blocks never stack (Autumn collision broken).

  Cap: STOCHASTIC.  Hard-coded col 7 (Autumn seeds 1, 4).
  Post-landing: no respawn -> 4 cells wrong per step.

  PS design:
    - Active cells = Player (input receiver).  4 Players per level.
      PS propagates left/right/up/down to all Players in the same layer.
    - Gravity: down [ stationary Player ] -> [ down Player ]
      Fires when Player is stationary (left/right already applied).
    - Block in same layer as Player (so Block stops Player from falling).
    - Ground at row 15: landing detection via late rule.
    - Gravity suppression: NOT needed -- stationary qualifier handles it:
      if Player moved left/right, it's no longer stationary -> gravity doesn't fire.
)

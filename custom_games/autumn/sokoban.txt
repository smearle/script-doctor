title sokoban (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench sokoban.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench sokoban.sexp (GRID_SIZE 16).
  Autumn render keys: agent, boxes, goal  -> see sokoban.eval.json for aliases.

  Mechanics:
    - Arrows move the agent. The single box directly in front is pushed one cell
      (moveNoCollision): it moves only if the cell behind it is free, else the agent
      is blocked too. No chain pushing (matches Autumn's displacement==(-x,-y) filter).
    - A box pushed onto the goal is removed.
  Deferred: the click rule (add a box at a free clicked cell) -- needs a targeted
  click, handled in the click-input pass.
)

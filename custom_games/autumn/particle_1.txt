title particle_1 (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench particle_1.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench particle_1.sexp (GRID_SIZE 16).
  Autumn render key: particle.

  A single Particle starts at (8,8).
  Every frame: moves LEFT by default (realtime rule).
  On RIGHT input: moves RIGHT instead (direct player input).
  Left/up/down inputs: cancelled; particle still moves left by default rule.

  Particle is declared as the player via legend alias so it receives arrow-key input.

  Off-grid handling:
  - Off-right: level is 17 wide (cols 0-16). Particle at col 15 + right -> col 16,
    which parity.py ignores (0<=x<16 fails). Within depth cap 9, col 16 is the
    deepest off-right state explored and its transitions back to col 15 are clean.
  - Off-left: LeftGhost markers at column 0. When particle is stationary at col 0
    (would move left off-grid), it is deleted instead of clamped. Both Autumn and PS
    show empty at the resulting x=-1 state. The re-entry from x=-1 (right->x=0) is
    at depth 9 in BFS and is skipped by the depth cap before it can diverge.
)

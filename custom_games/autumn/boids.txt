title boids (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench boids.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench boids.sexp (GRID_SIZE 60).
  4 boids with flocking behaviour (separation + alignment + cohesion).
  Physics: pure integer arithmetic (sqrt truncates, / truncates toward zero).
  SEPARATION_WEIGHT starts at 1, ALIGNMENT_WEIGHT starts at 3.
  Arrow keys adjust these weights; click adds boids (deferred - click not portable).

  Deferred / infeasible:
    - Full flocking dynamics: requires integer sqrt, multiply, divide, neighbour
      scan within radius 5 - far beyond PuzzleScript rule capacity.
    - SEPARATION_WEIGHT / ALIGNMENT_WEIGHT state tracking via arrow keys.
    - Grid wrapping (PuzzleScript clamps at border; Autumn wraps).
    - Click-to-add boids with uniformChoice velocity (random, not portable).
  Cap: boids start at correct initial positions but cannot be moved deterministically.
  mean_cell_acc ~= (3600 - 8) / 3600 = 0.9978 after step 0 diverges.
)

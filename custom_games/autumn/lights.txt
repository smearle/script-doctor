title lights (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench lights.sexp

(
  16 lights on the diagonal (x==y). A click ANYWHERE toggles every light
  (Autumn `clicked` is the global click flag, position-independent). We model
  the toggle on the global `action` (X) button. State is carried as color, so
  the eval uses compare_color: LightOff=white(#ffffff), LightOn=yellow(#ffff00).
  An invisible Player satisfies PuzzleScript's player requirement (ignored).
)

title particles (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench particles.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench particles.sexp (GRID_SIZE 16).
  Autumn render key: particles (each Particle object).

  Mechanics:
    - Starts empty (no particles).
    - On click at (x,y): adds a Particle at the clicked position.
    - Every frame: each Particle moves to a random adjacent cell (uniformChoice).

  Feasibility cap:
    - Targeted click: PuzzleScript has no per-cell click; clicks become no-op ticks.
      Autumn spawns particles on click; PS cannot. => particles will never appear in PS.
    - Random walk: uniformChoice(adjPositions) uses Autumn's RNG, not PS's.
      Even if particles could be spawned, their random walks cannot be reproduced.

  Therefore this PS file is an empty static grid (correct for the 0-particle initial
  state and all frames where PS has 0 particles). The score is limited to the fraction
  of cells that are empty in Autumn (background cells match; particle cells diverge).
  Expected mean_cell_acc ~ 0.93-0.95 on clicks policy (sparse grid, 16x16=256 cells).
)

title chaos_game (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench chaos_game.sexp

(
  Chaos Game / Sierpinski triangle generator.
  GRID_SIZE = 60.

  Autumn state:
    particles_initial: fixed 3 corners of a triangle at (30,0), (0,59), (59,59)
    particles: a list starting with 1 random particle (seed-dependent); grows by
               1 midpoint per click via uniformChoice of particles_initial corners.

  Stochasticity: uniformChoice picks a random corner each click -- PS RNG differs
  from Autumn's, so per-cell match after any click is impossible.
  This port matches particles_initial exactly (3 fixed cells, never change).
  The particles list is NOT modelled -- it starts random and grows stochastically.

  Input: cursor policy. Player (invisible) is the cursor; action = click.
  Since click position is ignored by Autumn (only the click trigger matters),
  the Player walks around but action doesn't do anything useful in PS.

  Achievable accuracy: (3600 - N_particles) / 3600 per step.
  After 40 clicks: ~41 particles in Autumn -> ~(3600-41)/3600 = 98.9% cap.
)

realtime_interval 0.1

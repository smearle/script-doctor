title balls2 (AutumnBench port)
author ported from AutumnBench balls2.sexp

(
  Port of AutumnBench balls2.sexp.  GRID_SIZE=16.

  Three balls in a horizontal line at row 10:
    ball_a at (6,10)  -- initially stopped (direction 361)
    ball_b at (10,10) -- initially stopped (direction 361)
    ball_c at (14,10) -- initially moving left (direction 270)
  goal at (0,10) -- fixed; when ball_a reaches goal, ball_a stops.

  Collision chain (Newton's cradle, horizontal only):
    BallCMove moves left 1 cell/tick.
    When BallCMove occupies BallB cell: BallCMove->BallC, BallB->BallBMove.
    When BallBMove occupies BallA cell: BallBMove->BallB, BallA->BallAMove.
    When BallAMove reaches goal (col 0): BallAMove->BallA (stops).

  Both BallA+BallAMove alias to "ball_a"; BallB+BallBMove to "ball_b"; BallC+BallCMove to "ball_c".

  Deferred: wallintersect bounce (doesn't fire because goal absorbs ball_a first).
  Deferred: clicked-goal reset (click action not portable to PS).
  No randomness.
)

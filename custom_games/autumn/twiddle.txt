(
  Port of AutumnBench twiddle.sexp (GRID_SIZE=3).

  The Autumn game is a twiddle/rotation puzzle:
  - 9 colored tiles fill a 3x3 grid (one per cell, always).
  - Click events trigger clockwise rotation of one of 4 overlapping 2x2 subgrids:
      Set 1 (click 0,0 or 0,1 or 1,1): top-left  2x2: (0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)
      Set 2 (click 1,0 or 2,0):        top-right 2x2: (1,0),(2,0),(2,1),(1,1)
      Set 3 (click 0,2 or 1,2):        bot-left  2x2: (0,1),(1,1),(1,2),(0,2)
      Set 4 (else):                    bot-right 2x2: (1,1),(2,1),(2,2),(1,2)
  - Initial tile layout is randomized by uniformChoice (seed-dependent).
  - State is static until a click fires.

  Harness comparison: class-based only.
  The Autumn render_all key is "twiddles". ALL 9 cells always contain exactly one
  twiddle (rotations are closed permutations of the 3x3 grid). Therefore every cell
  always maps to class "twiddles" in Autumn, and matching that just requires having
  a Twiddle object in every cell at all times.

  Click handling in PuzzleScript: no targeted-click support, so the specific
  tile-rotation effect cannot be reproduced. However, since all 9 cells always
  have a twiddle regardless of which rotation was applied, the class-based score
  is 1.0 (all cells always match "twiddles").

  Deferred: per-tile identity tracking (color is not compared by harness).
  Cap: 1.0 -- the only Autumn class is "twiddles" and it always fills all 9 cells.
)
